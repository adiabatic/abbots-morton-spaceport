JSON IS NOT THE BOTTLENECK:
  266.7 MiB of shards: json.dumps 0.79 s CPU, json.loads 1.05 s CPU (whole surface).
  Rate 0.0026 s CPU/MiB dumps, 0.0030 s/MiB loads (C encoder, mostly long strings).
  unit-cache store: 62148 json.dumps + gzip = 0.87 s; read back = 0.66 s.
  Total JSON+gzip across a warm rebuild ~= 3.4 s of 17.8 s CPU (19%);
  across a cold serial build ~= 2.4 s of 136.7 s CPU (1.8%).
