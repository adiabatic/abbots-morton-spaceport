# Cutover plan: the rebuild builds every font

The cutover of `doc/rebuild-design.md` §13.6: the rune files and the pipeline under `rebuild/` build Senior, Junior and Mono, `make all` runs that build, and the old engine and the comparison against it leave the tree. It prepares a release and makes none: the version, `README.md`, `FONTLOG.md` and the specimen pages' prose stay as they are.

## 1. Rulings

The author's, binding on every stage:

- **The blanks in the review queue do not gate the cutover**, and neither does a leak gate, which the rebuild has no counterpart to. §13.6's acceptance list is rewritten to match in stage 7.
- **The comparison against the old font retires.** The baseline tables, the oracle, the divergence ledger, the alias map and the old-against-new review corpus go, with git as their record. The review app's Queue then shows the accepted rebuild against an edit (`rebuild.review.build` against a snapshot), and the kerning view stays.
- **The replacement for the review corpus is built before the old one is deleted** (stage 4).
- **A pin the rebuild disagrees with is marked pending**: it keeps the author's text and carries `data-expect-pending` with a short reason, the suite reports it as an expected failure and fails it once it passes, the build's pin gate counts it without failing, and `WHATNEXT.md` points at the pending pins for a ruling. The pending pins are the four regroupings in `site/extra-senior-words.html` (`·Owe ~x~ ·Fee | ·It` twice, `·I ~x~ ·Fee | ·Utter`, `·No | ·Utter.alt`), the Manual's `·Utter ~x~ ·May | ·Thaw`, and the Manual's `·It ~b~ ·Zoo.half ~b~ ·I`.
- **`|` keeps its isolation meaning**: the text before the break shapes the same alone. Settlement's lookahead reads across a break, which is why `·Utter ~x~ ·May | ·Thaw` is pending.
- **A variation sequence selects its stance's shape, unjoined**, in the rebuilt Senior. Forced stances that join are an open fork for `WHATNEXT.md`, and the Manual pin that types VS2 for a joining half ·Zoo is pending on it.
- **The milestone names and the directory are renamed** (stage 6). The proposal for the directory is `engine/`.
- **Each stage is committed on master once its gates are green**, without a pause for approval.

Settled before the cutover and carried through it: ss02 leaves the font and ss08 joins it, and the old font's ·He-before-ZWNJ kern (`he-before-noentry` in `glyph_data/opentype-features.yaml`) is not carried (`rebuild/KERN-PLAN.md` §1).

## 2. Stages

`var/keep/cutover/` holds the research each stage's design rests on: the three surveys and the designs for stages 2, 3 and 4.

Each stage ends with `make test`, `make test-rebuild` and a `make artifact-cycle` pass green, and its commit.

1. **The whole Senior font from the runes.** `run_m1` compiles it, Regular as `rebuild/out/m1/M1.otf` and Bold beside it as `M1-Bold.otf`, with every glyph, the metadata, the variation sequences and the Latin `kern`, `ccmp` and `mark` features the shipped Senior has, and the gates run on those bytes. The old build still writes `site/`.
2. **Mono and Junior from the runes.** `rebuild/pipeline/build_family.py` builds both, Regular and Bold, from the rune files and the non-Quikscript glyph files, and reads nothing of `glyph_data/quikscript.yaml`: Mono takes each letter's `mono:` bitmap and Junior its isolated cell, with the alternates the variation sequences name, the gapped ·Owe behind ss06, ss10 and the namer dot, each derived from the registry. `make rebuild-fonts` (`rebuild/tools/build_fonts.py`) writes the six fonts under their shipped file names into `rebuild/out/fonts/`, Senior copied from `rebuild/out/m1/` once run_m1's last green build covers the inputs on disk. Nothing switches `make all`, and the old build still writes `site/`.
   - The four fonts differ from the old build's only as ruled (`rebuild/test_family_against_old_build.py`, which is why the old engine outlives this stage): they drop the glyphs no text reaches (Junior's ligatures, ·Vie+Utter among them, and the alternates' contextual siblings), the alternates and the gapped ·Owe take the names the rebuild's Senior gives them (`qsNo.flipped`, `qsUtter.alternate`, `qsOwe.sole.ss06`), and Junior's namer dot does not lower across a ZWNJ, by the rule shape Senior's emitter writes (`emit_gsub.namer_dot_lookup`). Every other glyph keeps its outline, advance and cmap entries.
   - `space` and the angle parentheses are unsuffixed records in `glyph_data/punctuation.yaml`, which compile the same in all three fonts and in the old build.
   - Mono and Junior need the spec load and no settlement table, so they build while the tables are red. The variation-sequence registry in `rebuild/script.yaml` carries a font scope: VS1 reaches all three fonts and VS2 only Senior. The Mono drawing of an alternate is the stance's base bitmap, which the build checks is five columns wide.
3. **The `data-expect` suite on the new fonts.** The grammar, the corpus collector and the checker are three leaf modules under `rebuild/validation/` (`expect.py`, `corpus.py`, `check.py`) that import nothing from `pipeline/`, `review/`, `tools/` or `test/`. The root conftest's pin items and the build's pin gate (`manual_pins`, which `run_m1` runs) call the one checker over all three pages: the suite against the rebuilt Senior and Junior in `rebuild/out/fonts/`, which its session builds first through `rebuild/tools/build_fonts.py`, and the gate against the font the build has just compiled, leaving out the Junior runs and saying so in its summary. `make test`'s key covers what those fonts are built from (`artifact_cycle.make_test_font_lines`), and the artifact cycle starts gate:make-test once the run_m1 gate passes. The old build still writes `site/`, which the old engine's tests read.
   - The checker reads a junction from the font's `curs` lookups and HarfBuzz's clusters, a base, stance, height and adjustment from the glyph name (a cell label, `check.parse_glyph_name`), and a trait, a bound bitmap and the isolated flag from the glyph-facts sidecar run_m1 writes beside the font as it mints the cells (`rebuild/pipeline/glyph_facts.py`), which `make rebuild-fonts` copies beside the Senior it publishes. A `|` between two letters asserts the break and that each letter shapes as it does with the text split there, compared with `kern` off.
   - The modifier vocabulary is the rune vocabulary (`doc/data-expect.md`, `check.MODIFIER_FORMS`): `.half`, `.alt`, `.exit`, `.extended`, `.en-ext-N`, `.ex-ext-N` and `.reaches-way-back` keep their text, `.noentry` is `.locked`, `.!baseline` is `.!ex-y0`, and a modifier outside the vocabulary is an error.
   - The pins that restate a ruling the runes carry read so: the three ss02 pins are under ss03, the two ·May pins carry no `.en-ext-1`, and `site/extra-senior-words.html` writes `·Vie ~b~ ·Utter`.
   - Of `test/test_calt_regressions.py`, the any-of `data-expect` checks are in `test/test_calt_expectations.py`, against the rebuilt Senior, and those the rebuild fails are strict expected failures naming what it shapes instead; the tests that read old names or anchors stay against the old build and go in stage 4, with the old harness `test/test_shaping.py` and `test/conftest.py`'s `shaping_env`. `test/test_namer_dot.py` compares bases.
4. **The comparison retires, `make all` switches, and the old engine goes.** The retirement is seven commits, each green, then the switch, then the old engine's removal.
   1. **Table-diff mode made ready for the cycle**, with nothing deleted. `var/keep/cutover/stage4-table-diff-design.md` is the design, with the existing code each part builds on; the tracked pointer departs from it as below.
      - `rebuild.review.build` builds a corpus of the current snapshot of the accepted build (`rebuild/review/snapshot.py`, format `ams-review-snapshot/3`, under `var/keep/review-snapshot/`) against `rebuild/out/m1`, and `rebuild.review.snapshot take` takes one. A snapshot records no commit, and its digest covers its format, the tables' stamp, the configurations and its files, so the same build taken anywhere gives the same digest.
      - A settlement row is a class-compressed rule over hundreds of windows, and one join change re-folds hundreds of rules, so a changed row is evidence and never a unit (`rebuild/review/diff_units.py`). A settlement unit is one cell change (the input rune, the old cell, the new cell) folded across configurations and left neighbors, whose left glyphs are evidence, a join unit is one (left, right, old, new) across configurations, and units that show the same text under the same configurations are one unit, in `joins` when any of them is a join row.
      - The candidate texts for a changed input are its old and new rules' certificates (the windows head holds one per rule, and the snapshot carries its own) and every text of at most three letters that holds the input, shaped in both fonts. A context that needs a longer text can be missed; diffing the window rows themselves is the sound fix and a fork for the author.
      - A glyph bucket diffs the snapshot's font against the new one per glyph both have (`diff_units.glyph_changes`: the decomposed outline, the advance and sidebearing, and the cursive anchors without their lookups, `fingerprint.glyph_facts`), because a bitmap, anchor or advance edit leaves the tables unchanged. Each redrawn glyph is a unit of the `glyphs` class over the shortest certificate that settles to it, else its code point alone (a Latin glyph's text, in the `non-quikscript` group), else a kerning-inventory text, else its letters under ss10; a glyph no text draws, such as a letter's bare glyph, shares one Queue card per letter. Glyph units merge with join and settlement units over the same text and configurations, after them in class precedence.
      - A kern bucket diffs `kern.tsv` by (leading side, following side) into units of the no-verdict `kerning` class, over the kerning inventory's witness of the pair, shaped, compared and drawn with kerning on (the fragment's `kerning`, `diff_units.KernedInkComparator`, the app's `kerned` samples). The kerning view stays where kern values are judged.
      - `cluster` is a digest of the unit's ink deltas, so the Queue (`static/queue.js` keeps only units with a `cluster`) groups by the pixels that change.
      - An empty diff, and a machine with no snapshot, build a zero-unit corpus the app boots on, whose manifest's `baseline` names the snapshot or says none is present.
      - The manifest's inputs fingerprint is `fingerprint.TABLE_DIFF_COMPONENTS` (the snapshot's record, the tables' stamp copied from the windows heads, the font inputs a kern-only rebuild answers for, the review code and the static files), and `make verdict-ready` on a table-diff corpus checks it, the after font byte for byte, a `snapshot` row (the current snapshot is the one the pointer names) and a `diff` row that never fails and counts the unaccepted changes, and fails a corpus that is not a table-diff corpus. `verdict_ready.diff_summary_line` is the cycle's summary line for the switch.
      - The tracked pointer `rebuild/review-snapshot.json` names the accepted snapshot by digest and records no commit: the accept writes it before the author commits, its change goes into the commit with the edit it accepts, and so the commit a snapshot stands for is the last commit that changed the pointer (`snapshot.pointer_commit`). The pointer names the snapshot of the build this commit lands with, the baseline the switch starts from, and a test holds its `snapshot_format` to the reader's, so a format bump takes the snapshot again in the same commit.
      - `make review-accept` (`rebuild/review/accept.py`) is a dry run unless `ARGS=--apply`. It refuses on a corpus that is not a table-diff corpus, while `make verdict-ready`'s blocking checks fail, while the corpus does not show `rebuild/out/m1`, while a pass holds the pass lock (`rebuild/tools/store_guards.py`), and while a human unit is blank, skipped, rejected or neither. `--apply` takes the snapshot, writes the pointer, empties the store under the store's lock beside a listening review server, and tells the open tabs to reload onto it; `--retake` takes it over the verdict, readiness and corpus refusals for a format bump or an unremakeable snapshot and says what span it accepts unreviewed.
      - `make review-snapshot-remake` exports the commit that last changed the pointer with `git archive`, runs that commit's own table build, glyph chain and `snapshot take` in the exported tree with this checkout's uv cache and a crate target of its own, and installs the result once its digest is the pointer's, or names the retake when it is not.
   2. **The cycle, the readiness check and `review-build` build the table-diff corpus.** `make artifact-cycle` and `make review-cycle` build it (`rebuild.review.build`) into `rebuild/out/review.next`, which the pass empties first, and land it, and `make review-build` builds it in place. The corpus build skips only when the served corpus is a table-diff corpus whose fingerprint (`status.table_diff_inputs` over the tables of `rebuild/out/m1`) and after font, byte for byte, a build would reproduce, so a kern-only pass rebuilds it (`artifact_cycle.corpus_build_skippable`).
      - On a stamp move the land carries the live store onto the new corpus by unit id under the store's lock (`verdict_store.carry`), so a verdict survives an edit that leaves its unit's content, and so its id, unchanged, a skip is dropped, and the rest go to `var/verdict-orphans/`.
      - The m1-audit store, its journal and stashes, and the last close-out's archive are kept as found under `var/keep/m1-audit-final/`, and the first table-diff land started from an empty store.
      - A green pass ends with a `review diff:` line (`verdict_ready.diff_summary_line`) above the readiness checklist. The diff is a row and a line, never a red gate.
   3. **The verdict store's writers are the review server, the land and the table-diff accept**, and its readers outside them the app, the readiness check and the triage export. The just-verdicted-now-what skill reads a review's rejects from the store and the corpus.
   4. **The verdict store is small and transient**: it holds the verdicts made since the last accept, and each writer writes it whole under the store's lock, the review server before it answers each save (`rebuild/review/verdict_store.py`). The accept empties it through the land's locked path (`landing.locked`) beside a listening review server and tells the open tabs to reload onto it (`ams:store/<snapshot digest>`).
   5. **The table-diff corpus is the only review corpus.** `rebuild.review.build` builds nothing else and takes no `--mode`; the review crate, `corpus_exec`, `units_exec`, `subset_pack`, the facts, the change groups, the drafters, feature reach, the unit stores, and the m1 fixtures and JS are out of the tree, and the cycle books the build's one process beside gate:conform's sweep (`artifact_cycle.CORPUS_BUILD_BYTES`).
   6. **The generic code has homes of its own.** The settle memo's keys, its code roster (`SETTLE_MEMO_CODE_PATHS`) and the staleness mask are `rebuild/pipeline/settle_memo_keys.py`, the shared memory segments the pools hand bytes home in are `rebuild/pipeline/shared_segments.py`, the build's alphabet is `rebuild/pipeline/alphabet.M1_ALPHABET`, and the conformance sweep's glyph-name check is `conform.check_glyph_names`. The memo's stamp hashes the roster's paths, so a pass after a move among them settles every window once.
   7. **The comparison against the old font is out of the tree**, with its tests, fixtures, docs and skills: the oracle and its row and position stores, `baseline_subset`, `rebuild/baseline/`, `rebuild/check_determinism.py`, the divergence ledger and the alias map with their checks, the old-font pin replay and the baseline row model in `rebuild/validation/`, the probe's baseline column, the kerning importer (the kerning view still reads the record it left, `var/kern/imported.json`), the add-a-new-letter skill and its evidence tool, the oracle's widths and job-costs rows, and the rebuild's reader of `glyph_data/senior_quikscript_kerning.yaml`. run_m1's gate is the defect gate and the Manual-pin gate, and `--gates-only` reruns those two over the build on disk, which saves a build for a contact allow-list bless and for an edit to the pin gate's code. The outside checks are the `data-expect` pins and the review corpus's diff against the accepted snapshot.

   8. **`make all` publishes the six fonts from the runes into `site/`** (`rebuild/tools/build_fonts.py`): Mono and Junior built from the rune files and the glyph files, and Senior copied from `rebuild/out/m1/`, whose build `make all` runs first when it is stale. The font suite's session publishes through the same command, never starting the build, and every test under `test/` and the pin pages checks the fonts there, with the Senior's glyph-facts sidecar read beside the build. The old engine's tests, `rebuild/test_family_against_old_build.py` and the Makefile targets that drove the old tools are out of the tree, and the rebuild suite reads no font under `site/`.
   9. **The old engine is out of the tree**, with its tools, skills, docs and site pages; commit `d23c0a87`, the last before the cutover, holds its code. `doc/abandoned-designs/demote-oracle.md` and `doc/abandoned-designs/guard-tables.md` are the chapters §13.6 names. `tools/` holds the generic font assembly the rebuild calls (`build_font.py`, `glyph_records.py`, `departure_mono_import.py`), the runes' reflow, the site server and the Manual letter survey's scripts, `glyph_data/` the runes and the other glyph files, and the variation sequences come from the registry alone.

   What the retirement gives up is the fixed outside reference: a mistake that is accepted becomes the reference. The `data-expect` pins are the outside check from then on.
5. **The moves the plans deferred to cutover.** The registry moves to `glyph_data/script.yaml` (`rebuild/M1-PLAN.md` §1), and `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` builds with a Rust toolchain.
6. **The renames.** The milestone-named things that are permanent (`run_m1`, `M1.otf`, `rebuild/out/m1/`, the `m1-*` ledgers that survive stage 4, `M1_ALPHABET`, the `m1_` lookup prefix) and the directory. The check before it: the review unit ids and the tables do not move.
7. **The tidy.** `AGENTS.md`, `WHATNEXT.md`, the skills and the docs lose what describes only the old engine or the finished migration, `doc/rebuild-design.md` and the plans state the layout as it stands, and the dead code and the note-taking violations the survey found go. This file is deleted with it.

## 3. Open forks

- **Forced stances that join.** The old font's `calt` goes on to join the stance a variation sequence selects; settlement reads only the raw cmap glyphs. Whether settlement gains forced-stance letters is the author's.
- **The seven ·Owe kern pairs** the importer never carried (it ran before ·Owe had a rune file), and the rulings `var/kern/import-report.json` lists, are the author's (`WHATNEXT.md`).
- **The namer dot before a ZWNJ.** The old font lowers the dot in namer dot, ZWNJ, Short letter, and the rebuild's Senior and Junior do not (`emit_gsub.namer_dot_lookup`'s `ignore sub`), which design §3.4 calls for. `rebuild/M1-PLAN.md` §2 says the opposite and is corrected in stage 7.
