# What next

## The main thread — M1 rune migration

The rebuild is migrating the cursive-join engine rune by rune, validating each batch against the old shipped font via the oracle.

**Current frontier: the qsZoo migration is built and awaits its sitting, with its qsThey_qsZoo ligature in the same batch.** `rebuild/M1-BATCH23-PROGRESS.md` holds its parked questions and verification recipe. The qsGay, qsThaw, and qsThey sittings remain open in `rebuild/M1-BATCH20-PROGRESS.md`, `rebuild/M1-BATCH21-PROGRESS.md`, and `rebuild/M1-BATCH22-PROGRESS.md`; delete each bounded scratch file when its sitting closes the batch. `make verdict-ready` is the standing check, `glyph_data/runes/` is the roster of migrated runes, and `make novelty-order` is the way into the blanks that remain.

**Where the state lives:** `make verdict-ready` — and the same status as a banner in the app — answers whether the surface, gates, and verdict store are ready: surface freshness by input fingerprint and against the M1 font under the served letters, gate greenness, verdict-store alignment, the frontier carry, blanks remaining, server. `rebuild/out/cycle_summary.json` is the last cycle’s own record (surface units and echo groups, unmatched oracle rows, each gate’s status, and the `verdicts-carried-<sha>.json` it wrote with how many verdicts rode it); the per-gate summaries under `rebuild/out/m1/` hold the rest (`conform_summary.json`, `manual_pins_summary.json`, `readback_summary.json`, `oracle_summary.json`); the store itself is `verdicts-autosave.json`, journaled in `verdicts-journal.ndjson`, which the newest verdicts ride until the next cycle’s carry; and `make complaint-docket` clusters the open reject/neither verdicts by the rune records that decided them. Read those; don’t copy their numbers back into this file, where they go stale the next time a cycle runs.

**The standing shape of the gates**, which is what a number here would have been trying to say: the crate's string replay holds every build's tables to its own engine over every string of the belt's universe (`run_m1.REPLAY_HORIZON`; `rebuild/out/m1/replay_summary.json` is its record), the conform belt is exact — zero divergences at horizon 4 over the settlement configurations, and over the ss10 overlay's two-letter arm, which read-back's isolation proof makes complete — and since the standalone boundary gate retired it carries the split-buffer check itself (`make conform-deep` carries it at 5+); read-back proves the font holds the planned rules and its boundary glyphs inert, and the build's witness stage proves every rule reachable by settling the certificate the crate wrote beside it; the Manual pins replay clean under the no-waiver gate, scope included; and the M1 GSUB rides type-7 Extension offsets by design (`m1_settle` is `useExtension`), inside the subtable-offset headroom floor read-back holds it to. The oracle’s remaining unmatched rows are verdict-gated divergences from the old font, not failures — among them the deliberate ss03 ·Fee·Tea divergence, where the rune extends by 1 per the old derive’s stated intent and the divergence lands through verdicts with no ledger entry. The docket’s standing complaints are windows already argued and accepted, so a _fresh_ complaint is the signal. The artifact cycle lands the echo prefill and the checked-in standing approvals (`rebuild/standing-approvals.yaml`) itself after its carry+merge, so only genuinely new questions ever queue — and echo groups key on the localized ink delta, which puts unchanged flanking letters, including followers that merely slid over by an advance change, outside the grain.
